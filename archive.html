Research Collections

AAA's Research Collections comprises material gathered from focused research projects undertaken by AAA in partnership with artists, art professionals, and researchers. It is a growing repository of primary source material, photographic documentation, video recordings, ephemera, and more. Since 2010, AAA has been digitising these valuable resources and making them accessible for users worldwide.
